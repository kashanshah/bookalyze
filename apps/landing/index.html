Questions

Frequently asked

When can I start using Bookalyze?

We're opening early access in stages. Join the waitlist and we'll email you as soon as your spot is ready.

Is it a replacement for my current bookkeeping app?

Yes. Bookalyze is full double-entry accounting with a chart of accounts, bank reconciliation, receipts and financial reports. If you use Wave, you can import your entire history, including receipts.

Which marketplaces and banks are supported?

Amazon (North America, UAE and more) first, then eBay and Noon. Wise syncs automatically, and any other bank works through statement uploads.

Can I manage several companies in different countries?

That's what it's built for. Each company has its own currency, tax settings, financial year and team, and you switch between them in a click.

Is my data secure?

Each company's data is isolated at the database level, connection keys are encrypted, and every change is recorded in an audit log. Two-step sign-in is on the way.

How much will it cost?

Early access is free. Paid plans will come later, with only the features you need.
